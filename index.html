A live partner in your online calls

During a call, Ownflow follows what is being discussed and works one step ahead. When an answer, a follow-up or a task is coming, it prepares the draft.

  1. It follows the call

    Ownflow listens from your Mac and keeps up with the topic and the decisions as they happen.

  2. It drafts what is next

    An answer to the question just asked, a follow-up email, the tickets that came out of the call. Ready before you need it.

  3. You steer, it finishes

    Read the draft. Comment on it or add your own input. Ownflow finishes it.

No bot joins the call, so nobody sees a guest in the meeting, and we don't store the audio. You tell the other people on the call that you are using Ownflow. The app doesn't. See how meeting notes work.

It knows your context

Good help depends on what Ownflow knows about the call. It draws on six sources.

  • Your calendar

    Who is on the call, and what it is for.

  • Past meetings

    What you agreed last time with the same people.

  • Your screen

    What you are looking at while you talk.

  • Linear

    The tickets your team is working on.

  • Notion

    The docs and notes you already keep.

  • Slack

    The threads your team is in.

Questions

Is Ownflow free?

Dictation is free and unlimited, runs on your Mac and needs no account. Meetings and AI features are paid plans that start at €15 a month. Everything is free for everyone during the test.

Is my dictation private?

Yes. Speech is recognized on your Mac and your audio never leaves it. If you turn on cleanup, the recognized text is sent for cleanup and not stored.

Does a bot join my calls?

No. Ownflow listens from your Mac, so nothing joins the call and nobody sees a guest in the meeting. The audio is sent to be transcribed and is not stored as a file. You tell the other people on the call that you are using it.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon, on macOS 14.4 or later.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. iPhone is coming soon.

Can I connect my own AI to Ownflow?

Yes. Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Cursor can read your meetings through a local, read-only server on your Mac. You choose what agents can see, every read is logged, and you can revoke a client at any time.

How much does Ownflow cost?

Free: dictation, unlimited, on your Mac. €15 a month: meetings and summaries. €30 a month: AI features and the proactive live agent. €90 a month: everything, unlimited. Free for everyone during the test.
