Meeting notes, without a bot.

If you use one app for dictation and another for meeting notes, Ownflow does both in one.

What leaves your Mac

Feature What leaves
FeatureDictation What leavesAudio never leaves your Mac. With cleanup on, the recognized text is sent for cleanup and not stored.
FeatureRewrite What leavesThe text leaves to produce the rewrite. The selection stays on your Mac.
FeatureMeetings What leavesThe audio leaves to be transcribed, and it isn't stored as a file. The transcript and the notes are stored with your account.
FeatureScreen question What leavesThe window and the question leave for that ask. Ownflow doesn't keep them.

Without an account, Ownflow is dictation only, and your dictation stays on the Mac. What's stored, and for how long, is in the privacy notice.

Dictation is free. Meetings and cloud features are invite-only for now.
