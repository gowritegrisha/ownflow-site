The free app, without an account

Dictation is recognised on your Mac. Without an account, the app makes only two kinds of request, and nothing else. It also opens a mail draft, but only if you ask.

  • The speech model download. The first run downloads the speech model, and choosing another model downloads that one. The file comes from Ownflow's releases on GitHub. If GitHub fails, it comes from Ownflow's model storage at Supabase in Frankfurt. Like any download, the server sees your IP address and the file asked for.
  • The update check. When it checks for updates, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and downloads it from GitHub if there is one. GitHub sees your IP address and the request.
  • A mail draft, only if you open one. Report a problem opens a draft in your own mail app. The draft holds the app version, the source SHA and the macOS version, and what you write. The app doesn't send it. You do, if you choose to.

The dictation audio, the dictation text, your history and your dictionary stay on the Mac. Without an account there is no sign-in, no cleanup, and no usage or diagnostic events. GitHub and Supabase are listed under who else processes it.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub receives your IP address and the page asked for. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics. If you ask for an invite with the form on the download page, your email address goes to Ownflow's database at Supabase, with a salted hash of your IP address so repeated requests can be limited. What the app is for and how long Ownflow keeps it say how they are used and when they go.

The controller is Grisha, as in who is responsible. The contact details are in the aviso legal. To ask about your data, or for help, use the support page. The same rights apply to you without an account.

What you are being asked to accept

The controller accepted the tester notice text, version 2026-11-02.1, on 2 Nov 2026. You tell the other people on the call yourself. The app won't tell them.

What the app is for
  • Sign you in with Google and keep your tester account.
  • Dictate on your Mac, including when the cloud is unavailable.
  • Send dictation text for cleanup, and selected text for rewrite, when you are online.
  • Record a call you start, transcribe it, and draft notes.
  • Answer a question about a window on your screen. That uses a vision model.
  • Count usage so the beta can stay on the current free plans.
  • Let you ask for help without attaching the contents of a dictation or a meeting.
  • You can ask for an invite by giving an email address on the Ownflow website. A tester can also suggest someone by email, up to three people. Ownflow stores the address, whether it came from the website or a tester, and when it was asked. Ownflow does not email that person. Grisha decides who is invited. A request is deleted when Grisha invites or declines it, or after 90 days, whichever comes first. The address is not used for anything else. A tester's suggestions are included in that tester's data export and are removed when that tester's account is deleted. The website form and the suggestion control stay off until Ownflow publishes this text and turns the feature on.

The first tester group is expected to have every current feature, including meetings. Screen questions use Groq qwen/qwen3.8-27b. When you ask, the app sends a screenshot of the front window you chose, with your question. It does not send the whole display. Work calls are out. Use it for your own calls and calls with friends. You tell anyone else on the call that you are recording. The app shows you a recording indicator and lets you pause, stop and discard, or drop the other person's channel. It does not notify them.

Who else processes it

Regions below are what the provider pages said on 1 Nov 2026, plus the region this project is configured to use. They aren't a claim that every vendor is inside the EU, and they aren't a claim that no vendor trains on data.

Supabase Pte. Ltd.

Role Database, sign-in storage, and the API. Also Ownflow's model storage, the second source of the speech models, used without an account too.

Region This project is in Frankfurt (eu-central-1). The Supabase DPA (version dated 1 June 2026) says data is stored and primarily processed in a region the customer directs, and that Supabase may still process it where a subprocessor or the law requires. The subprocessor list of 12 Oct 2026 names US hosting companies, including Amazon Web Services, Google and Cloudflare.

Training and retention The terms fetched 1 Nov 2026 say Supabase won't use customer data to train a model without prior written consent. The free plan has no daily hosted backup. Nightly dumps run on the operator's Mac. See the retention table.

Deepgram

Role Turns meeting audio into text. Ownflow calls api.eu.deepgram.com with mip_opt_out=true.

Region Deepgram's current data page says that EU host stays in the EU and doesn't fall back to another region. The specific EU country can change. Deepgram's privacy policy, last updated 26 Oct 2021, still says it stores data on servers in the US. Both sentences are on Deepgram's site. A short-lived token doesn't itself force the EU host or the opt-out; the app sets both.

Training and retention Deepgram's default is to keep audio and transcripts for model improvement. The opt-out flag is how Ownflow turns that off per request. Opted-out audio is described as kept only while the request is processed. Usage logs are still kept, and Deepgram says those logs are retrievable for 90 days. Deepgram's subprocessor page lists audio processors including Amazon Web Services, Oracle, Cloudflare, OpenAI and Anthropic.

Groq

Role Cleanup, rewrite, meeting notes, and screen questions. A screen question sends the screenshot and the question. The vision model is qwen/qwen3.8-27b. Cleanup, rewrite and notes tests use openai/gpt-oss-20b. That name isn't evidence that OpenAI sees the text.

Region Groq's data page says retained customer data is in Google Cloud buckets in the United States. The DPA allows processing in the US and other countries, with standard contractual clauses as the stated transfer tool.

Training and retention The services agreement, last modified 22 June 2026, says Groq may not train or fine-tune on inputs or outputs unless the customer allows it. When zero data retention is on, it stops Groq from logging inputs and outputs for reliability and abuse checks.

Google

Role Sign-in only, for this beta. Scopes are openid, email and profile. The Google app is in Testing, for at most 100 test users, on GCP project ownflow-513418.

Region Not pinned to Frankfurt by Ownflow.

Training and retention Not restated here. Google isn't the model provider.

GitHub

Role Hosts the app downloads, the update feed and the first copy of the speech models, in Ownflow's public ownflow-releases repository. Also used without an account.

Region Not pinned to Frankfurt by Ownflow.

Training and retention GitHub receives the download requests, with your IP address. It gets no dictation, account or meeting data from Ownflow.

The evidence, with the page addresses, is in the processor register that ships next to this file in the Ownflow source. Provider lists change. The provider pages were read on 1 Nov 2026. The 2 Nov changes are the vision model, zero data retention, the Mac backups, and account export and deletion.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy, and to object. You can also complain to a supervisory authority. The practical path today:

  • You can delete a meeting in the app. Stop-and-discard drops that recording.
  • Full account export and full account deletion are in the app, in Settings, on the connection screen. Export downloads your account. Deletion asks you to type delete and to sign in with Google again. A backup can still hold the database copy for up to seven days. A restore replays deletions, as the row above says. Other Macs are not wiped from the Mac where you delete.
  • You can still ask for help through the support page. The operator can help.
  • Other people on a call are not users of Ownflow. They ask you, the person who recorded. You can pause, stop and discard, or delete their channel.
What this notice doesn't do
  • It doesn't authorise work calls.
  • It doesn't make the app announce a recording.
  • There isn't a hidden mode. The panel steps aside when the screen is captured. It isn't invisible.
  • It doesn't accept Supabase, Deepgram or Groq terms or DPAs on the controller's behalf.
  • It doesn't claim the service is EU-only.
  • It doesn't add an analytics product. Diagnostic detail is versions, error codes and timings, not contents.
