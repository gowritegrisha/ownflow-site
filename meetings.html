One meeting, three moments

  1. During the call
  2. Right after
  3. Later

During the call

Press Cmd+Shift+M to start. It records your microphone as Me and the call audio as Them, on your Mac, with no bot joining. Use headphones. Ownflow warns you when the sound is going to the speakers.

A recording pill stays on screen the whole time. If someone objects, pause, stop and discard, or delete the Them channel right there. Text you dictate with push-to-talk stays out of the meeting record. A marker shows where it happened.

Me12:04The launch slips if onboarding isn't ready.
Them18:22We can ship Thursday if the copy is locked.

Right after

Your typed notes, the transcript and a template become structured notes. Your own words are kept as you wrote them. Edit the result, or make the notes again. The earlier version is kept.

The transcript follows the languages on the call. The notes are written in English for now, whatever language the call used.
