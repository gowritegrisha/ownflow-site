Download Ownflow

Dictation on your Mac is free. It needs no account and no sign-in.

Download for MacApple silicon, macOS 14.4+

Open the disk image and drag Ownflow to Applications. This test build is not notarized yet, so the first time macOS says it cannot check the developer. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then Open. macOS asks once.

The first run walks you through the microphone, Accessibility and the speech model download. After you allow Accessibility in System Settings, click Ownflow in the menu bar to go back to it.

What changed in each version is in the release notes. Test builds do not update themselves: download the newest one from this page.

Meetings and the cloud

Sign in with Google in Ownflow to turn on meeting notes, cleanup and rewrite. No invitation is needed while the test is open, and it is free. The test has room for 100 people.

It's for personal calls. You tell the other people. The app doesn't.
